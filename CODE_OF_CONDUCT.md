# Quevaa Code of Conduct

Quevaa welcomes contributors who want to improve private, offline-first
women's health technology with care and respect.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively
- Assume good intent while staying clear and accountable
- Be considerate of privacy, health, cultural, and reproductive experiences
- Keep public discussions free of private health data
- Give and receive feedback with professionalism
- Respect maintainer decisions about roadmap, safety, architecture, licensing,
  and product direction

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized, violent, or degrading comments
- Publishing another person's private information
- Pressuring users or contributors to disclose health, fertility, pregnancy, or
  intimate information
- Deliberately undermining privacy, safety, or security processes
- Misrepresenting unofficial forks, builds, or services as endorsed by Quevaa

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to:

kenoyek@gmail.com

If a report also involves a vulnerability or possible exposure of sensitive
health information, use the security contact in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, block
participants, or take other reasonable steps to protect the project and its
community.
