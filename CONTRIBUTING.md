# Contributing to Quevaa

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Quevaa.

Quevaa is a source-available, privacy-focused women's health, cycle-tracking,
productivity, and wellness application. Community contributions are welcome
while the project's intellectual property, product direction, and commercial
rights remain protected.

## Ways to Contribute

You can help by:

- Reporting bugs
- Reproducing issues
- Improving tests
- Improving accessibility
- Fixing defects
- Improving performance
- Improving documentation
- Suggesting features
- Submitting pull requests

## Before Building a Major Feature

Please open an issue before implementing significant architectural or product
changes. This prevents contributors from spending substantial time building a
feature that may conflict with Quevaa's architecture, safety requirements,
roadmap, or product direction.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests should:

1. Clearly explain the problem being solved.
2. Avoid unrelated changes.
3. Include tests where appropriate.
4. Pass Flutter analysis and existing test suites.
5. Follow the existing Quevaa architecture.
6. Preserve Quevaa's offline-first and privacy-first principles.
7. Avoid introducing external health-data services without prior discussion.

## Contributor Rights

You must only submit material that you have the legal right to contribute.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that the contribution is subject to the
Quevaa Contributor License Agreement in `CLA.md`.

A contribution may not be merged until the required contributor agreement has
been accepted.

## Community Standards

By participating in Quevaa, you agree to follow the community standards in
`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
